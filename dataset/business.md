# Casa Aurora: reference information (fictional)

Casa Aurora is a small retreat house in the hills. This is the only information the assistant may use to answer a guest. It is invented for this project and describes no real business.

- Check-in from 15:00. Check-out until 11:00.
- The stay includes three vegetarian meals a day, tea and coffee. Vegan meals are available if requested when booking.
- Rooms: private double room, 90 euros per night per room; shared room with four beds, 55 euros per night per person.
- A 30% deposit confirms a booking (card link or bank transfer). The balance is paid on arrival.
- Free cancellation up to 14 days before arrival. After that, the first night is charged.
- Location: 25 minutes by car from the village of Vale Verde. There is no public transport to the door. The house does not offer airport transfers; after a booking is confirmed, the team can send the phone number of a local taxi company.
- Pets are not allowed.
- Children over 8 are welcome in private rooms.
- Wi-Fi is available in common areas only, not in the rooms.
- The team speaks Portuguese, English, Spanish and Russian.
- Quiet hours: 22:00 to 07:00.
- Availability is checked by the team in the booking calendar, which the assistant cannot see.
